# Code of conduct

Vulcanary uses the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its community standard.

Be respectful, specific, and security-conscious. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private source or credentials, and disruption of good-faith collaboration are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and may result in warnings, contribution restrictions, or removal from community spaces.

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issue trackers, and public spaces where someone is representing Vulcanary.
