# Vulcanary name and marks

The MIT license covers the source code; it does not grant rights to the Vulcanary name, canary-and-forge logo, or other brand assets.

You may truthfully describe unmodified software as “Vulcanary” and say that compatible software works with Vulcanary. Forks and modified distributions must use a distinct product name and logo, and must not imply endorsement or official status.

Community articles, reviews, screenshots, and links to the official project may use the name nominatively. Commercial branding, merchandise, domains, or services using the Vulcanary marks require prior written permission.

This lightweight policy protects users from confusing unofficial binaries with official releases while keeping ordinary open-source discussion easy.
